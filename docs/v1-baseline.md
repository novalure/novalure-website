## Public route inventory

The site redirects `/` to English and has DE, EN and ES variants. V1 exposes home, developers/promoters, agents/brokerages, playbooks, project check/contact, system example, thank-you states, imprint/legal, privacy and cookie routes. Canonical route mapping and `hreflang` are centralized in `lib/i18n.ts`. Sitemap and robots are generated by App Router routes.

Primary route families:

- `/en`, `/de`, `/es`
- `/en/developers`, `/de/bautraeger`, `/es/promotores`
- `/en/agents`, `/de/makler`, `/es/agencias-inmobiliarias`
- `/en/playbooks`, `/de/playbooks`, `/es/playbooks`
- `/en/contact`, `/de/kontakt`, `/es/analisis-del-proyecto`
- `/en/system-example`, `/de/systembeispiel`, `/es/ejemplo-del-sistema`
- Localized legal, privacy, cookies and thank-you routes

## Forms and integrations

- Contact/Project Check: client and server validation through `/api/contact`
- Playbook selection and delivery: `/api/playbook`, double opt-in confirmation at `/api/playbook/confirm`, Resend delivery and HubSpot-compatible fields
- Booking: HubSpot meeting link/embed is held behind the external-media consent category
- Consent: necessary, analytics, marketing and external-media categories; optional tracking is gated
- Analytics placeholders: GA/GTM, Meta, LinkedIn and HubSpot identifiers are optional environment-driven integrations
- SEO: localized metadata, canonical URLs, `hreflang`, Open Graph/Twitter metadata, sitemap, robots and structured data

Environment variable names were inventoried without reading or documenting secret values. The names cover public site/Sanity configuration, HubSpot forms and meeting URLs, Resend, Upstash/KV, double-opt-in signing, playbook URLs and optional analytics identifiers.
